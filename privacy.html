Privacy Policy for Hexblast

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Hexblast ("the App"), developed by GG Games, handles information when you use the App.

Information We Collect

Hexblast does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All game data - including your score, level progress, settings, and daily mission progress - is stored locally on your device using browser local storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

Permissions

The App requests device vibration access solely to provide haptic feedback during gameplay (e.g. when popping tiles). No data is collected or transmitted through this permission.

Third-Party Services

Hexblast does not currently integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. If this changes in the future (for example, if advertising is added), this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly, and any such service's own privacy practices will be disclosed here.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, as no data collection occurs at all.

Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, uninstalling the App or clearing its storage will permanently delete all associated data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
